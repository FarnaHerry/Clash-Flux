# Clash-Flux v0.3.15

## 简体中文

- 统一代理、订阅、规则与日志页的二级分区切换：内容可跟手横向滑动，取消时回弹，切回分区保留列表位置。
- 改进标签栏跟手效果：下划线在拖动中延伸移动，标签条同步滚向目标标签并尽量居中，反向与回弹时跟随；手动浏览标签不会被拉回。
- 优化大分区滑动性能，避免隐藏虚拟列表每帧触发可见页面布局；修复快速反向切换时 Pager 离场页丢失的问题。
- 日志的内核来源和应用来源分别保留滚动位置；滚动至二级标签两端后可以继续切换一级页面。

### 支持边界

手机端保持单活动代理订阅。桌面订阅编排能力与兼容边界见项目文档；iOS 仍处于 TODO 阶段，不提供发布包。

## English

- Standardize secondary section navigation across Proxies, Profiles, Rules and Logs. Content follows horizontal dragging, rebounds when canceled and retains its scroll position when revisited.
- Improve tab tracking: the underline stretches and moves during a swipe, while the tab strip follows the destination toward the center. Reversals and rebounds stay synchronized, and manual tab browsing remains in place.
- Improve swiping with large sections by preventing hidden virtual lists from invalidating visible layout every frame. Fix Pager departing-page loss during rapid reverse navigation.
- Preserve separate scroll positions for core and application logs. Swiping past either end of a nested tab group can continue to the adjacent top-level page.

### Scope

Mobile retains one active proxy subscription. Desktop subscription orchestration and compatibility limits are documented in the project. iOS remains TODO and has no release package.
