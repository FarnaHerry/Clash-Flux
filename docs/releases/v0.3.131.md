## English

- The default theme pairs neutral gray surfaces with four-color gradients on selected navigation indicators and icons. Selected cards get a four-color outline and a short sweep. Reduced motion uses a static outline.
- Home traffic totals mark downloads in blue and uploads in amber. The group and node pickers switch directly, and their arrows resume from the displayed angle during rapid toggles.
- Continuous scrolling in vertical proxy groups rebuilds fewer node cards around the viewport.
- Android versionName is 0.3.131 and versionCode is 208.
