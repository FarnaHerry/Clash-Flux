# Clash-Flux v0.3.54

## 简体中文

- 自动选择等不能手动切换节点的分组保留正常卡片外观与交互；点击节点卡会提示该分组仅作展示，测速和当前状态仍可查看。
- 原生 VPN 连接配置采用只读卡片状态：不能设为主订阅，但连接勾选框和管理操作仍可用；点击卡片会说明用途。

## English

- Groups that do not allow manual node selection, such as URL-test groups, keep the normal card appearance and interaction. Clicking a node explains that the group is for display; latency and current status remain available.
- Native VPN connection profiles use a read-only card state: they cannot become the primary subscription, while their connection checkbox and management actions remain available. Clicking the card explains its purpose.
