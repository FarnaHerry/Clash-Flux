# Clash-Flux v0.3.41

## 简体中文

- 首页流量统计改用新版圆角饼图。上传和下载都为零时，图表按两项等分，中心显示 `0%`；有流量后显示真实比例。
- 统一首页、托盘和设置入口的内核运行操作状态，避免快速重复操作造成状态错乱；网络端口、局域网和 IPv6 等配置集中在设置页，日常模式、系统代理与 TUN 控制留在首页。
- 连接列表改为响应式卡片，在窄窗口保持易读布局；修复连接项延迟渲染时读取组合环境导致窗口崩溃的问题。
- 设置页整理为通用设置、网络参数、平台维护与关于，减少重复的运行控制入口。

## English

- The home traffic summary now uses the rounded pie chart. When upload and download totals are both zero, the two slices are equal and the center shows `0%`; positive totals display their actual shares.
- Home, tray, and settings now share core-operation state to prevent conflicting rapid actions. Network ports, LAN, and IPv6 remain in Settings, while everyday mode, system proxy, and TUN controls are on Home.
- Connections use responsive cards that remain readable in narrow windows. Fixed a crash caused by reading composition-only resources when a connection item was rendered later.
- Settings is organized around general preferences, network parameters, platform maintenance, and About, with fewer duplicate runtime controls.
