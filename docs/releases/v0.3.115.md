## English

- Extend rule-level `no-resolve` handling to RULE-SET references, GEOIP, and IP-ASN conditions, and preserve CIDR modifier semantics in a restricted set of positive AND conditions. When a rule-set snapshot proves that resolution is unnecessary, the ordinary pre-resolution step can be skipped. Reverse GeoIP rules now require an existing destination address, preventing unresolved domains from matching through inversion. AND conditions short-circuit in order, preserving the use of already-resolved addresses by later no-resolve conditions.
- Complex AND/OR/NOT, rule sets with mixed resolution needs, and provider logic that cannot be verified remain conservative and are recorded in the fidelity ledger. This release does not claim full fidelity for complex logic. Matching multiple destination addresses differs between sing-box and Mihomo, so the relevant mapping remains approximate.
- Android versionName is 0.3.115 and versionCode is 193. iOS remains deferred without release assets.
