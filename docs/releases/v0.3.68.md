# Clash-Flux v0.3.68

## 简体中文

- 修复新增订阅成功后列表没有及时显示，以及删除成功后旧卡片仍留在界面、再次删除提示“订阅不存在”的问题。
- 修正订阅列表在切换、拖动或页面切换期间丢失更新的情况；失败的主订阅切换会恢复持久化的选中状态。

## English

- Fixed subscriptions failing to appear promptly after import, and deleted cards remaining visible until a second attempt reported that the subscription did not exist.
- Fixed subscription list updates being lost during profile selection, reordering, or page changes. Failed activation now restores the persisted selection.
