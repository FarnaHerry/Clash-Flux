## English

- Extend rule-level `no-resolve` to OR/NOT logical rules and eligible classical rule sets. The compiler checks the complete logical tree and candidate rule-set snapshot before skipping resolution. Mixed resolution needs and provider logic that cannot be verified retain their conservative behavior and fidelity accounting.
- Subscription downloads try pinned Clash (Mihomo) and then sing-box user agents; only HTTP 403 advances to the next identity. Settings can reverse the order. The Clash-Flux fallback identity is removed. Pins do not follow app or upstream releases, and changing the order affects future downloads without rewriting saved profiles.
- Home system proxy and TUN gear controls now share their hover, press, and hit areas. On wide proxy pages, the speed-test overlay no longer shortens the scrollbar track.
- Android versionName is 0.3.116 and versionCode is 194. iOS remains deferred without release assets.
